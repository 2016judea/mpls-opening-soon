201 Minneapolis businesses about to open, and about to hire

A business that doesn't exist yet has no staff yet. These are the Minneapolis restaurants, shops, clinics, studios and offices building out a new space, taking out a new liquor license, or getting their first health inspection. 43 are at final inspection or waiting on a liquor license, the last step before the doors open, and 49 more are under construction.

    Sources, all public: the City of Minneapolis building permit register, on-sale liquor license list and food inspection records, pulled Oct 17, 2026, and business filings at the Minnesota Secretary of State.

    How each signal is read. A build-out counts when the permit requires a new certificate of occupancy, changes the occupancy, or names a new use or tenant; roof, restroom, stair and spec-suite work is left out. Opening soon: the permit is at final inspection, or a new liquor license is pending. A new liquor license is one among the newest the city has issued (numbers run in sequence). A first health inspection means the kitchen had none on record since January 2024. Just opened: the permit closed in the last 60 days, or one of the other two signals with no open permit. The owner is the manager or chief executive on the state filing for the name the city uses; blank when no exact match came back.

    Minneapolis only. Saint Paul stopped publishing permits in mid-2025. From Aidan Jude at Brick & Mortar (aidan@brickandmortar.dev).
